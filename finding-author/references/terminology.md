# Terminology

Resolve terminology after the Finding Model's facts, decisions, conclusions, and selected type payload are complete.

Search only for concepts already present in the model. Prefer project and dependency vocabulary, then controlling standards or specifications, official technical documentation, authoritative security taxonomies, and primary research or advisories.

Adopt a specialized term only when its reviewed definition matches the finding, improves precision over ordinary technical language, and has a known applicability boundary. Resolve competing definitions only when the difference would change the finding's meaning. Record the term, meaning, applicable claim, source, and boundary in `terminology`.

Terminology cannot add a mechanism, security property, failure mode, consequence, severity, responsibility, or remediation. It cannot make a finding sound more serious. When no exact term exists, retain precise ordinary technical language rather than inventing a label.

Terminology is complete when every adopted term has a reviewed definition and applicability boundary, and further research would not change the wording materially. There is no source-count target.
